## Hints

- Should raw card numbers ever touch your servers? What does that imply for compliance?
- The charge succeeded at the provider, but your server crashed before saving the result. How do you find out?
- How do you represent money in code and in the database?

## Answer

**1. Requirements:** card and wallet payments for orders, refunds, multiple currencies, no double charges, a full audit trail, PCI DSS compliance, and support for SCA/3-D Secure (EU).

**2. Use a payment service provider (PSP)** like Stripe, Adyen or Braintree, with **hosted fields, Payment Element or redirect checkout**. Card data goes straight from the browser to the PSP, so your scope stays minimal (PCI SAQ A). Never log or store PANs or CVVs.

**3. Flow (intent-based)**

```text
1. Client: "Pay for order 123"
2. Server: verifies cart and amount server-side (never trust client prices),
   creates Payment(PENDING) and a PSP PaymentIntent with an
   Idempotency-Key = payment.id; returns client_secret
3. Client: confirms with the PSP SDK (3-D Secure challenge if required)
4. PSP -> webhook: payment_intent.succeeded / payment_failed
5. Server: verifies webhook signature, dedupes by event id,
   transitions Payment -> SUCCEEDED, marks order PAID, emits OrderPaid
```

- **Webhooks are the source of truth**, not the browser redirect (the user may close the tab). Make handlers idempotent, accept events out of order, and return `2xx` quickly while processing asynchronously.
- **Explicit state machine:** `PENDING -> REQUIRES_ACTION -> SUCCEEDED | FAILED`, then `REFUNDED` or `PARTIALLY_REFUNDED`. Reject invalid transitions.
- **Idempotency** on every call to the PSP and on your own endpoints, so retries never double-charge.

**4. Data and money**
- Store amounts as **integers in minor units** plus a currency code (`4999`, `"EUR"`). Never use floats.
- Keep a **double-entry, append-only ledger** for balances, payouts and refunds. No in-place edits. Corrections are new entries.
- Write payment events and order updates with a **transactional outbox**.

**5. Reliability and operations**
- **Reconciliation jobs:** compare your records with PSP reports and balance transactions daily, and query the PSP for payments stuck in `PENDING`.
- Timeouts plus status polling, retries with backoff, an alert on mismatches.
- Fraud tools (PSP risk scoring), rate limits on payment attempts, and handling of disputes and chargebacks.

**6. Security:** TLS everywhere, webhook signature verification, secrets in a vault, least privilege, and an audit log of who refunded what.

**Follow-ups:** "Authorize now, capture at shipping?" (separate auth and capture, and holds expire), multiple PSPs for redundancy, and subscriptions with retries for failed renewals (dunning).
